An illustrative GTM framework

How a growing agency might structure the decision to bring digital media services in-house.

MARKET OPPORTUNITY

Existing relationships

  • Identify client demand for integrated media services
  • Assess services lost to external suppliers
  • Prioritize client and vertical fit
VALUE PROPOSITION

More control and speed

  • Integrated campaign planning and execution
  • Faster optimization and clearer measurement
  • Consistent service ownership
  • Incremental agency revenue from expanded digital media services
COMMERCIAL MODEL

Services that scale

  • Launch core programmatic, search, and social services
  • Define scope and pricing principles
  • Expand with repeatable client demand

Illustrative lifecycle: Validate demand → Stand up capability → Prove results → Scale responsibly

GTM ≠ Marketing Only

Operating capabilities

  • Platform selection and vendor evaluation
  • Campaign trafficking, optimization, reporting
  • Client onboarding and workflow standards
  • Talent recruitment, management, training

Measures to track

  • Digital services revenue and client adoption
  • Managed spend and operational workload
  • Gross margin and delivery capacity
  • Retention, service quality, and campaign outcomes
