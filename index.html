An illustrative GTM framework

How a growing agency might structure the decision to bring digital media services in-house. These are illustrative strategic components, not the agency’s original internal documents.

MARKET OPPORTUNITY

Existing relationships

  • Identify client demand for integrated media services
  • Assess services lost to external suppliers
  • Prioritize client and vertical fit
VALUE PROPOSITION

More control and speed

  • Integrated campaign planning and execution
  • Faster optimization and clearer measurement
  • Consistent service ownership
COMMERCIAL MODEL

Services that scale

  • Launch core programmatic, search, and social services
  • Define scope and pricing principles
  • Expand with repeatable client demand

Illustrative lifecycle: Validate demand → Stand up capability → Prove results → Scale responsibly

GTM ≠ Marketing Only

Operating capabilities

  • Platform selection and vendor evaluation
  • Campaign trafficking, optimization, reporting
  • Client onboarding and workflow standards
  • Talent recruitment, management, training

Measures to track

  • Digital services revenue and client adoption
  • Managed spend and operational workload
  • Gross margin and delivery capacity
  • Retention, service quality, and campaign outcomes

How the capability can be built

A sample phased roadmap based on the kind of work involved in the transformation. Milestones are illustrative; this is not a reconstruction of actual dates.

PHASE 01 · VALIDATE

Business case & technology

  • Confirm demand within current accounts
  • Evaluate programmatic, search, social platforms
  • Define service economics and offer scope
PHASE 02 · LAUNCH

Hands-on execution

  • Lead initial campaign delivery
  • Build reporting, QA, and optimization routines
  • Document client workflows and delivery standards
PHASE 03 · SCALE

Team & operating model

  • Recruit and train specialists
  • Standardize planning and delivery processes
  • Monitor growth and operational capacity

The core thesis: Launch with capable hands-on execution, then institutionalize what works in systems and teams.
